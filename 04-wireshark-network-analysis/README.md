# Wireshark Network Traffic Analysis

**Status:** Planned  
**Level:** Beginner

## Objective
Learn packet-capture basics and recognize common protocols and unusual traffic patterns.

## Tools
Wireshark, safe sample PCAP or traffic generated on your own network

## Lab Steps
1. Open a safe sample PCAP or capture traffic you are authorized to inspect.
2. Identify protocols such as DNS, TCP, TLS, and HTTP when present.
3. Apply display filters and inspect packet details and conversations.
4. Record observations and explain what the capture cannot tell you.
5. Avoid labeling encrypted traffic malicious without supporting evidence.

## Evidence to Add
Include the capture source, filters, protocol observations, relevant packet numbers, and sanitized screenshots.

Suggested additions after completing the lab:
- `screenshots/` — screenshots captured from your own environment
- `notes.md` — observations and explanations
- `analysis.md` or `incident-report.md` — investigation summary, where appropriate

## Questions to Answer
- What were you trying to find out?
- What did you do, and why?
- What did you observe?
- What does the evidence support, and what does it not prove?
- What would you investigate next?

## Safety and Accuracy
Capture only traffic you are authorized to inspect. Remove sensitive details from screenshots where appropriate.

Do not mark this project Complete until you have performed the steps yourself, checked the results, and added your own evidence.
