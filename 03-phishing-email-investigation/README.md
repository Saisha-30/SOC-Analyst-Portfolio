# Phishing Email Investigation

**Status:** Planned  
**Level:** Beginner

## Objective
Practice examining a suspicious email and documenting evidence safely.

## Tools
Training email sample (.eml), email headers, text editor, reputable analysis resources

## Lab Steps
1. Use a training sample or clearly identified public example.
2. Inspect sender and reply-to details, authentication results, URLs, domains, attachment names, and wording.
3. Do not click suspicious links or run attachments.
4. Record indicators and explain which observations are suspicious, benign, or inconclusive.
5. Recommend safe user and analyst actions.

## Evidence to Add
Document the sample source, relevant header fields, indicators, reasoning, confidence, and recommended next steps.

Suggested additions after completing the lab:
- `screenshots/` — screenshots captured from your own environment
- `notes.md` — observations and explanations
- `analysis.md` or `incident-report.md` — investigation summary, where appropriate

## Questions to Answer
- What were you trying to find out?
- What did you do, and why?
- What did you observe?
- What does the evidence support, and what does it not prove?
- What would you investigate next?

## Safety and Accuracy
Do not upload private emails or confidential headers to public analysis services. Never open unknown attachments for this exercise.

Do not mark this project Complete until you have performed the steps yourself, checked the results, and added your own evidence.
