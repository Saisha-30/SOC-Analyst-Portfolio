# Threat Intelligence and IOC Investigation

**Status:** Planned  
**Level:** Beginner

## Objective
Learn what indicators of compromise are and how to research them with context and caution.

## Tools
Public threat reports, reputable threat-intelligence sources, spreadsheet or text editor

## Lab Steps
1. Select an IOC from a reputable public report or safe training dataset.
2. Record its type, source, context, and any dates provided.
3. Compare information from more than one source when possible.
4. Explain confidence, false-positive risks, and what an analyst should validate before escalation or blocking.

## Evidence to Add
Include the IOC type (not live interaction), source references, context, confidence limitations, and recommended validation steps.

Suggested additions after completing the lab:
- `screenshots/` — screenshots captured from your own environment
- `notes.md` — observations and explanations
- `analysis.md` or `incident-report.md` — investigation summary, where appropriate

## Questions to Answer
- What were you trying to find out?
- What did you do, and why?
- What did you observe?
- What does the evidence support, and what does it not prove?
- What would you investigate next?

## Safety and Accuracy
Do not visit suspicious domains directly or download malware. Reputation scores are context, not proof.

Do not mark this project Complete until you have performed the steps yourself, checked the results, and added your own evidence.
